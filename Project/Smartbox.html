Smart Medicine Box

An Iot based device that reminds patients to take their medicines on time



Project Overview

The smart Medicine box is an IOT based device that remainds patients
to take their medicines on tim. It uses sensors and a microcontroller
to provide audio and visual alert.

Objective

  1. Remind user to take medicine on time
  2. Provide audio and visual alerts
  3. Easy to use and affordable
